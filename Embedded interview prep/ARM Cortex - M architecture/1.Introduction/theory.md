## Arm Cortex-M

Arm is a company that designs CPU cores.

Cortex M series is one of the series of CPU cores designed by Arm.

Examples:
- Cortex M3
- Cortex M4

---

## Difference bw Cortex-M, Cortex-A and Cortex-R

Each of it is designed for various purposes.

- M is for Microcontrollers. STM32F411 has Cortex-M4 core
- A for application processor used in tablets, raspberry pi etc
- R for real time processor ex: automobile systems

---

## Cortex-M4

The Cortex-M4 is a 32-bit RISC processor that follows the ARMv7E-M architecture.

---

## Arm Cortex-M4 is not a Microprocessor

Cortex-M4 is not a microprocessor. It is a 32-bit CPU core based on the ARMv7E-M architecture.

Chip manufacturers integrate the Cortex-M4 core with memory and peripherals to create microcontrollers such as STM32F411.
